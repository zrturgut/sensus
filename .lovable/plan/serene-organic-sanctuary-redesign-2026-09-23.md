# Serene Organic Sanctuary Redesign

## Outcome
Refine Sensus into a dark, calming mindfulness sanctuary without changing its existing features or workflows.

## Visual changes
- Replace the grid and neon ambience with a midnight navy background and slow sage, lavender, and sky aurora drift.
- Restyle shared panels, insight cards, dialogs, goal tools, and vision surfaces as soft frosted glass with warm hairline illumination.
- Shift typography toward relaxed, warm-white body text with an elegant Lora accent for reflective statements and affirmations.
- Increase spacing, rounded corners, and line height across desktop and mobile.
- Give the microphone a gentle idle pulse and an eight-second meditative recording breath cycle.
- Warm the Daily Affirmation area with subtle amber and rose light rather than a bright animated ring.

## Technical details
- Update semantic color, shadow, radius, and font tokens in the global stylesheet.
- Add three fixed decorative aurora layers behind the application, with reduced-motion support.
- Keep all current controls, persistence, voice recording, analysis, audio, calendar, and vision-board behavior unchanged.
- Validate the clarity and vision views at desktop and mobile sizes, including the recording visual state.
